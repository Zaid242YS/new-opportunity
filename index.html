1Welcome

The Furas Tree turns one Arabic word, فُرْصَة (fursah, an opportunity; plural فُرَص furas), into a living tree you can climb. Under the Creed-to-Commerce™ principle, everything we do in business and life flows from a creed and stays accountable to it. So the roots are the Creed, the branches are behaviors in Deen and Dunya, and the fruit is Commerce carried by Barakah.

The app helps you do four things:

  • See the full map of opportunity paths in Arabic with English parallels.
  • Choose one concrete behavior on each branch and track it from Planned to Mastered.
  • Learn from ten scholars of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama‘ah whose teaching or life improved ordinary people’s lives.
  • Report your growth as a PDF, and keep, back up or clear your data at any time.
The app is an educational reflection tool. It is not a fatwa, and it does not replace a qualified teacher.

2Quick start (5 minutes)

  1. Open the file in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) on phone, tablet or computer.
  2. On the Tree tab, tap the gold-bordered trunk and read what فُرْصَة means.
  3. Tap any pulsing box. It is the next step on that branch. Read the definition and the “Behavior to practice.”
  4. Press Planned if you will start soon, or Practicing if you have started. Add a short note and a target date.
  5. Go to My Journey, type your name and this season’s intention (نِيَّة).
  6. When you are ready to start fresh, open Report & PDF and press Remove sample data.
The app starts with a few sample entries so you can see how it looks. They are tagged “sample” or “SAMPLE:”. Remove them once you understand the screens.

4The Tree tab

Reading the picture

  • Dark chips at the base of each stem name the branch. Green chips are Deen; brown-gold chips are Dunya.
  • Level labels (Awaken, Strive, Excel, Benefit Others) appear in the narrow centre column.
  • A pulsing box is the lowest unfinished level on that branch, your suggested next step.
  • On a narrow screen, the tree scrolls sideways inside its frame. You can also switch to the outline.

Tree view and Outline view

Use Tree view for the picture and Outline view for a list of collapsible branches with each level’s status. Phones open in Outline view automatically.

The detail panel

Tap any box (or a root, the trunk or the crown). The panel shows:

  1. The branch, realm and level.
  2. The Arabic term, English parallel and definition.
  3. Behavior to practice, a concrete, measurable action.
  4. Guiding voice, a link that jumps to that scholar’s card.
  5. Status buttons, a Target date and My note. Everything saves as you type or tap.
Choose only one or two behaviors at a time. Mastering L1 on three branches beats starting L1 on all ten.

5My Journey tab

Your name and intention
Type your name and a sentence of intention. Both appear on the PDF report.
Progress & Balance
Counts of boxes by status, and two bars: Deen % and Dunya %. If the gap is more than 10 points, the app suggests which side to strengthen so that livelihood stays rooted in Creed, and faith is expressed in useful work.
Your Next Steps
One row per branch showing the lowest unfinished level and its behavior. Tap a row to open that box on the Tree tab.
Opportunity Log
Record real opportunities: date, realm (Deen or Dunya), the opportunity spotted, the action taken, and the outcome or lesson. Press Add entry. Use Edit on any row to change it (then Save changes or Cancel edit), or Delete to remove it.
A good log entry is specific: “Client asked for a rush job → wrote clear terms and price → accepted; learned to confirm deadlines in writing.”

6Ten Scholars tab

Ten cards, in rough historical order: Al-Hasan al-Basri, Abu Hanifa, Ibn al-Mubarak, Imam al-Shafi‘i, al-Shaybani, al-Mawardi, Imam al-Ghazali, Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim and Ibn Rajab. Each card shows dates and role, their contribution to everyday lives, their main work, a saying or example with its source, and which tree boxes they guide.

Authenticity tags

TagMeaning
QuotedWording widely reported from the source named on the card.
AttributedA verse or saying ascribed to the scholar, such as a line from a diwan.
Teaching summaryA paraphrase of the book’s theme, not a direct quotation.
Lived exampleA biographical example from classical sources, not a saying.
Always verify the exact wording against the cited source with a qualified teacher before teaching, publishing or selling content based on these cards.

8Your data & privacy

  • Everything you enter stays in your own browser (local storage). The app does not send it anywhere.
  • Data does not follow you to another browser, device or private window. Use Save my data and Load data to move it.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data erases the app’s data as well. Keep a backup file.
  • If you change a sample box (status, note or date), the app treats it as yours, and “Remove sample data” will no longer delete it.
“Clear ALL my data” cannot be undone. Save a backup first if you may want it later.
Because it is private to the browser, avoid entering sensitive personal details if you share a device.

11Troubleshooting

The Arabic looks plain or the font is different.

The Amiri font loads from Google Fonts. Offline, the browser uses a standard Arabic font. The content is identical.

Download PDF does nothing, or opens the print window.

The PDF tools load from the internet when the page opens. If they could not load, the app opens the print dialog instead. Choose “Save as PDF” there, or reload the page while online and try again.

My progress disappeared.

Data is tied to one browser. You may have opened the file in another browser or a private window, or cleared site data. Use Load data with your latest backup file.

I cannot see the whole tree on my phone.

Swipe the tree sideways, or press Outline view, which is easier on small screens.

I tapped “Remove sample data” but some samples remain.

Samples you edited are treated as your own work. Reset them to Not started by tapping the status again, or use Clear ALL my data if you want a completely blank start.

A box says “Not started” but is pulsing.

That is expected: the pulse marks the next step on the branch, whatever its status.

How do I move my data to another device?

Press Save my data, send the .json file to the other device, open the app there and press Load data.

12Using it with adab

  • Renew your niyyah each time you open the app. The tree is a means to action, not a scorecard to show off.
  • Do not measure yourself against others. Compare today’s self with yesterday’s.
  • Treat scholars’ words as guidance, and take rulings that affect your trade, finance or family to a qualified teacher.
  • Remember the balance of Qur’an 28:77: seek the Hereafter with what Allah has given you, and do not forget your share of this world.
Educational use only. Not a fatwa, legal or financial advice.
